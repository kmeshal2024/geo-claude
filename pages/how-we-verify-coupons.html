How SmartCopons Verifies Coupon Codes

Methodology last updated: {{DD Month YYYY}}

Every coupon on SmartCopons goes through the same checking process before it is published. Here is exactly how we do it.

1. Sourcing

We collect codes from three sources: official retailer promotions, verified affiliate networks, and reader submissions. We do not scrape and re-post codes blindly.

2. Testing

Before a code is published, a member of our editorial team adds a qualifying item to the retailer's cart and applies the code at checkout to confirm the discount actually applies. We record the discount amount and any conditions (minimum spend, new-customer-only, category limits).

3. Dating

Each published coupon shows two dates: the date we last verified it and its expiry date. The "Last verified" stamp at the top of every store page reflects the most recent review of that store's codes.

4. Re-checking and removal

We re-check active codes on a regular cycle and whenever a reader reports a failure. Codes that no longer work are removed or clearly marked as expired — we do not leave dead codes on the page to inflate our listings.

5. Corrections

Found a code that didn't work? Tell us via our contact page and we'll re-verify it quickly.
