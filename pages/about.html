About SmartCopons

SmartCopons is a coupon and discount-code platform that helps shoppers in the UAE, the Gulf region, and worldwide save money at online stores. We publish verified promo codes, voucher codes, and deals — every code is tested and dated before it goes live.

Our mission

Coupon sites are full of dead codes. SmartCopons exists to fix that: we test codes, remove the ones that don't work, and stamp each coupon with a "Last verified" date so you know it works before you click.

How we work

  • We source codes directly from retailers and their official affiliate programs.
  • Each code is tested at checkout before publishing.
  • Every coupon shows a verification date and an expiry date.
  • Codes that fail our check are removed, not left up.

Read the full process on our How We Verify Coupons page.

Who we are

SmartCopons is run by a small editorial team focused on the UAE and Gulf online-shopping market. Our job is simple: keep the codes on this site working and current so you never waste time on an expired deal.

How we make money

SmartCopons is free for shoppers. When you use some of our links or codes, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. This never affects whether a code is listed — we only publish codes that we have verified.
