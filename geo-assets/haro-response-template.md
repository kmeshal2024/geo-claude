# Journalist-Request (HARO / Qwoted / Featured) Response Template (Phase 5, Item 22)

Responding to journalist requests earns mentions on high-authority news sites — the
kind of source AI answer engines trust and cite. Monitor: Featured (formerly HARO),
Qwoted, SourceBottle, and UAE journalist requests on X/LinkedIn.

## Response formula (keep under 200 words)
1. One-line credential (who you are, why you're a valid source).
2. The actual answer — quotable, specific, with a number if possible.
3. A short bio line + link.

## Template

> **Re: {{query topic, e.g. "expert tips on saving money online in the UAE"}}**
>
> I'm {{Your Name}}, {{title}} at SmartCopons (smartcopons.com), a UAE coupon platform
> that verifies discount codes daily.
>
> {{2–4 sentences of genuinely useful, specific advice with a concrete number or
> example. E.g.: "The biggest mistake UAE shoppers make is buying at list price during
> White Friday week — our data shows the deepest electronics discounts actually land
> 3–4 days AFTER the headline sale, once retailers react to competitors. Always compare
> the code-applied price, not the 'was' price."}}
>
> Happy to provide more data or a fuller quote.
>
> **Bio:** {{Your Name}}, {{title}}, SmartCopons — https://smartcopons.com
> Contact: {{email}}

## Rules
- Answer the exact question; don't pitch the brand hard — the link in the bio does that.
- Lead with a specific number or contrarian insight; generic tips don't get picked.
- Respond fast (within a couple of hours) — journalists work on deadline.
- Never fabricate data. Use real numbers from your own coupon dataset.
