# Trustpilot / Review-Profile Setup (Phase 5, Item 20)

Why this matters for GEO: AI engines answer "is X legit?" by reading review
platforms. SmartCopons currently has no Trustpilot footprint — that's a citation gap
and a trust gap. Fixing it feeds both the AI answer and the E-E-A-T signal.

## Setup checklist
- [ ] Claim the free Trustpilot business profile for `smartcopons.com`.
- [ ] Complete the profile: logo, description, company info, website link, category
      ("Coupons & Discounts").
- [ ] Verify domain ownership.
- [ ] Add the same company details you put on the /about page (consistency = entity clarity).
- [ ] Also claim/complete: Google Business Profile (if a physical presence),
      Sitejabber, and a Scamadviser profile response (address the flags it raised —
      add company name, contact, address to the site so its score improves).
- [ ] Add `Organization` schema `sameAs` links to the new profiles.

## Earning first reviews (do NOT fake reviews — it violates Trustpilot & is detectable)
- [ ] Add a post-interaction email/prompt asking real users who saved money to review.
- [ ] Add a "Review us on Trustpilot" link in the footer and post-copy-code modal.
- [ ] Respond to EVERY review within 48 hours (templates below file).
- [ ] Target: 20+ genuine reviews in 90 days to establish a rating AI can cite.

## What "good" looks like for citation
- A visible star rating with a real review count.
- Owner responses on reviews (shows an active, real business).
- Consistent NAP (name/address/phone) across Trustpilot, the site, and schema.
