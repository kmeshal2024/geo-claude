# Original Data Study — Content Brief (Phase 5, Item 23)

Original data is the single best asset for earning citations and backlinks: journalists
link to it, other sites quote the stat, and AI engines cite the source of the number.
SmartCopons sits on proprietary coupon data — turn it into a study.

## Working title
**"The UAE Discount Report {{Year}}: Average Savings by Category, and When Deals Are
Actually Deepest"**

## The angle (why it gets cited)
A dated, UAE-specific, numbers-first report that answers questions people and AIs ask:
- What's the average discount by category (fashion, electronics, travel, beauty, food)?
- Which months/sale events have the deepest discounts (White Friday, Ramadan, DSF, back-to-school)?
- How much can a typical UAE shopper save per year using codes?

## Data to pull from your own platform
- Average discount % per category (from your active coupons).
- Distribution of discount sizes (how many codes are 10% / 20% / 50%+).
- Discount depth by month (time-series across the year).
- Top stores by number/size of verified codes.
- % of codes that are new-customer-only vs everyone.

## Deliverables
1. A pillar page: `https://smartcopons.com/uae-discount-report-{{year}}/`
   - H1: "UAE Discount Report {{Year}}"
   - Key findings as a bulleted, quotable summary at the TOP (AI-citable).
   - 4–6 charts (embed as images with descriptive alt text + a data table below each —
     tables are machine-readable and citable).
   - "Methodology" section (sample size, date range) — this is what makes it credible.
   - `Dataset` + `Article` schema.
2. A one-paragraph "key stat" pull-quote for outreach (e.g. "UAE shoppers saved an
   average of {{X}}% on electronics in {{year}}, with the deepest cuts in {{month}}").
3. A press/outreach email built around the headline stat (reuse Email 5 in
   outreach-emails.md).

## Distribution
- Pitch the headline stat to UAE business/lifestyle press (see target-publications.csv).
- Post the key chart on social with the stat.
- Update it annually so it stays the current, citable source.

## Why this beats another coupon page
AI engines cite the ORIGIN of a statistic. If "the average UAE electronics discount is
X%" traces back to SmartCopons, your brand gets named in the answer — the ultimate GEO win.
